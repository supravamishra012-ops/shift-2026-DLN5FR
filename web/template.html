/ About

About DRISHTI

Why shared e-micromobility struggles to take off, and how DRISHTI helps planners and riders close the gap.

The challenge

Demand and safe, serviced supply do not meet

Short trips are most common in dense neighbourhoods. Those are also the places with the least shared service, the least safe riding space and the busiest traffic.

Research question

What DRISHTI sets out to answer

How should a shared e-micromobility service provider locate its hubs, battery-swap stations and riding corridors to capture the most short-trip demand that is safe and practical to serve?
How it works

Locate, connect, power, ride

Each workspace in DRISHTI builds on the one before it.

Locate

Neighbourhoods are scored on demand, access, safety and need. An optimisation places hubs where they serve the most people by road.

Connect

Hubs are linked by calm routes on the real road network that avoid high-stress and arterial roads.

Power

Battery-swap stations are sited for the hubs they serve, with charging needs and van loops sized together.

Ride

Riders plan door-to-door journeys: walk to a hub, ride the calm route, and walk to the destination.
